Widgets

Put Roosevelt's orders and proclamations on any page of trlibrary.com, or anywhere else. Add one script tag, then drop in a card. Each card loads live from the API, links back to the full record, and keeps its styles to itself so it won't disturb the page around it.

<script src="https://executiveactions.labs.trlibrary.com/widgets/executiveactions.js" defer></script>

One document

A card for a single order or proclamation, by its id. Ids appear in every document page's address.

<trpl-action id="proc-563"></trpl-action>

Signed on this day

What Roosevelt signed on today's date, in any year of his presidency, biggest first. If nothing carries today's date, it shows the next date that does. Set date="MM-DD" to fix a date.

<trpl-actions-on-this-day count="3"></trpl-actions-on-this-day>
<trpl-actions-on-this-day date="06-08" count="4"></trpl-actions-on-this-day>

A filtered list

Any mix of subject, state, year and type. Without a year, it shows the most significant documents first, listed in date order; with a year, the earliest first. A “See all” link opens the same filter in the index.

<trpl-actions-list category="forests" state="Arizona" year="1908" limit="10"></trpl-actions-list>
<trpl-actions-list category="monuments" limit="5" theme="dark"></trpl-actions-list>

A story teaser

A short card that sends readers to one of the stories, by number or slug.

<trpl-story n="2"></trpl-story>

Story teasers appear here once the first stories are published.

Options

AttributeWidgetsWhat it does
themealllight (default), dark, or auto to follow the visitor's system setting
ctaalloff hides the “Plan your visit” link
data-apiallPoint at another copy of the API, such as a staging site. Can also go on the script tag
idtrpl-actionDocument id, e.g. proc-563, eo-1003, uo-018
date, counttrpl-actions-on-this-dayFixed MM-DD date; how many to show (default 3, up to 12)
category, state, year, typetrpl-actions-listFilters. Subjects use the slugs in categories/index.json; states use plain names; type is executive_order, proclamation or unnumbered_order
limit, sort, min, headingtrpl-actions-listHow many (default 10, up to 50); date or significance; lowest weight to include, 1–5; your own heading
n or slugtrpl-storyStory number or slug

Fonts follow the page when it sets --ea-display, --ea-serif and --ea-sans; otherwise the cards use the Library's brand fonts with sensible fallbacks.
